# Lesson format

## Weekly rhythm (default, ~2.5 hours)

| Slot | Length | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session A ("Learn") | 30 min | The ideas, with analogies and a live demo they follow |
| Session B ("Play") | 30 min | Guided exercises in a real tool |
| Session C ("Use it") | 30 min | Apply it to their own life or work |
| Weekend challenge | 60 min | One thing they make and keep, earns the badge |
| Sunday check | 5 min | Five-question quiz, glossary review |

Fill in their days from the intake. If they have less time, merge A and B; never drop the hands-on parts.

## Session template

```
### Session A — <title> (<day>, 30 min)
Why this matters: <one line tied to their life>
The idea: <short explanation, one analogy per new term>
Try it now: <numbered steps in a named tool>
You should see: <what success looks like>
If it didn't work: <the most likely fix>
New words: <term — plain meaning>
```

## Weekend challenge

A single task with a visible result, a time box, and a "show someone" step (send it to a friend, show family). Name the badge they earn. Offer an easier and a harder version.

## Quiz

Five questions: three multiple choice on ideas, one "spot the mistake" in a short claim, one "what would you do" scenario. Do not show answers until they reply, then explain each wrong answer in two sentences.

## Glossary

Running list, alphabetical, one line each, carried forward every week.

## Tone

Second person, warm, brief. No "simply" or "just" in front of a step that might be hard for them. Celebrate the badge in one line, not a paragraph.
