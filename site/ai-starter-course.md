# ai-starter-course: learn AI without buying a course

A self-paced AI course for people who are not technical, run by your own AI assistant. It starts with how AI actually works — what an LLM, a token, a parameter and a GPU are, and how Claude or ChatGPT turns your question into an answer — and ends with nine agents you built and a website you put online yourself. It will not sell you anything, promise that an AI trading bot makes money, or hand an agent your passwords.

## What you get

| Week | What you learn | What you make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | How AI and LLMs work, in plain words | A "how AI works" explainer for a friend |
| 2 | Tools for images, voiceovers, video and slides | A 30-second promo |
| 3 | Using Claude or ChatGPT in daily life | A library of 10 reusable prompts |
| 4 | AI at work: finance, trading, manufacturing, design, writing | A before/after of one real task |
| 5 | What agents are and how they're built | A morning-briefing agent |
| 6–7 | Agent lab | Eight more everyday agents, each passing a safety checklist |
| 8 | Vibe coding and going live | A live website with a link |

Each week has three short sessions, a weekend challenge, a five-question quiz and a badge. You get the full schedule and Week 1 straight away; later weeks arrive when you say you're ready. Tool names and free tiers are looked up when each week is written, so the course doesn't go stale.

## Install

```
npx skills add arm-pz/ai-starter-course
```

Or copy the folder to `~/.claude/skills/ai-starter-course/`, or upload the zip in the Claude app under Settings → Capabilities → Skills. Then say: "I'm not technical — run me an AI starter course."

## Questions

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Code shows up only in Week 8, and the AI writes it; you describe what you want and test it.

**Do I need to pay for tools?**
No. The course picks free options first and tells you when something needs a paid plan.

**How is this different from topic-primer?**
topic-primer teaches one concept in depth. ai-starter-course is a scheduled, hands-on course on understanding and using AI.
